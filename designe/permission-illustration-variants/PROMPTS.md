# Варианты иллюстраций разрешений Atlas

Три пары концептов; слева — местоположение, справа — уведомления. Каждый PNG 1774 × 887, с прозрачным фоном. Это варианты для выбора; текущие иллюстрации приложения не заменены. Для внедрения выбранную пару следует подготовить как два отдельных квадратных ассета и проверить в экранах разрешений.

Использован встроенный image_gen, без CLI/API fallback. Промпты ниже сохранены полностью.

## 1. Лаконичные символы

Файл: [01-minimal-location-notifications.png](./01-minimal-location-notifications.png).

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one paired concept presentation board for Atlas mobile app permission onboarding. Generate a wide 2:1 composition, ideally 2048x1024, with exactly TWO SEPARATE large isolated 3D illustrations in equal left and right halves, no divider, no labels, no text, no phone UI, transparent background with true alpha. LEFT: location permission symbol, a bold cobalt blue rounded ceramic map pin hovering above a small pearl-white circular map disc with one gently curved blue route and tiny endpoint; make it simple, visually distinctive, premium, readable at 180px. RIGHT: notification permission symbol, a bold cobalt blue ceramic ringing bell with two restrained pale-blue curved sound marks, hovering above one pearl-white softly rounded blank message card with a small cobalt dot. This is direction 1: refined minimal object illustrations without faces or mascots, fewer elements and more visual breathing room than typical busy onboarding art. Match the two sides as one consistent family: same scale, same three-quarter camera, soft bright studio lighting, smooth satin-gloss polymer and ceramic, rounded chunky geometry, cobalt/electric Atlas blue (#2877FF), icy pale blue and warm white, subtle local contact shadows only, gentle blue bounce light. Keep subjects entirely inside their halves with at least 10% padding. Both compositions occupy similar height and weight. No large scene background, no gradients filling the canvas, no human hands, no lettering, no logo, no watermark, no padlocks, no green or yellow accent. Transparent cutouts suitable for both light and dark app screens. High quality original 3D render.
```
