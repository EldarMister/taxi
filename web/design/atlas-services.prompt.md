## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: premium 3D brand illustration for the Atlas mobility app landing page, inspired by the sculptural lavender product artwork of aave.com.
Primary request: One beautifully art-directed group of three sculptural physical objects that communicate a city app with rides, parcel delivery, and food. Objects: a smooth thick three-dimensional navigation route ribbon forming an elegant angular A-shaped loop, a compact rounded parcel cube with one subtle embossed A chevron, and a very simple elegant handled takeaway bag with the same embossed chevron. This is a sophisticated product render, not a cartoon icon set.
Composition: a single tight interconnected asymmetrical arrangement, navigation ribbon large in back left, parcel cube low in front center, bag upright on right, slight overlapping but clear silhouettes. Orthographic three-quarter view, high quality studio render. Fill 85% of a wide 3:2 canvas, no cropping.
Materials: substantial extruded forms with precise rounded chamfers; satin lilac ceramic, milky frosted translucent lavender resin, and clear glass edges. Restrained pale lavender #B8A8F8, deeper #8F7DE6 shadows, warm white highlights. All three share the same refined material and perspective. Detailed ambient occlusion and realistic soft grounding shadows, broad studio softbox lighting from upper left. No neon glows.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha. Keep subtle natural contact shadows as transparent shadows below objects.
Constraints: NO text, NO letters other than the purely abstract angular A chevron form, NO screens, NO app mockups, NO smartphones, NO people, NO cars, NO road maps, NO stars, NO extra floating confetti, NO generic blue plastic, NO gradients in a rectangular background. A single polished production asset ready to place on a pale lavender website.
